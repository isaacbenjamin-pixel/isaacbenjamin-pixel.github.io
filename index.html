DANI

DANI is a personal desktop assistant for one user. It reads and manages that user's own Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Tasks and one Google Sheet, at the user's request, on the user's own computer.

Privacy policy

DANI accesses Google user data only for the person who signs in, only to show and manage their own calendar, mail, tasks and notes inside the app. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that user's computer. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

DANI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
